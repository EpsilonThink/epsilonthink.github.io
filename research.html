Research

My research is in theoretical condensed matter physics, with an emphasis on collective quantum phenomena and ways of controlling them through interfaces, interactions, geometry, and the surrounding environment.

Superconductivity

I work on conventional and unconventional superconductivity, including interface-enhanced superconductivity, microscopic pairing mechanisms, and superconducting heterostructures.

Quantum magnetism and spintronics

I am interested in spin transport, magnon generation, superconducting spintronics, and the coupling between magnetic and superconducting systems.

Topological quantum matter

My work also includes topological phases of matter, higher-order topological insulators and superconductors, and the relation between topology and experimentally observable boundary phenomena.

Disordered and amorphous systems

A further direction concerns electronic structure, multifractality, and interaction effects in disordered and amorphous quantum materials.
