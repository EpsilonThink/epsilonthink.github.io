Research

We study collective quantum phenomena and ways of controlling them through interactions, interfaces, geometry, disorder, and the surrounding environment.

Superconductivity

We work on conventional and unconventional superconductivity, including interface-enhanced superconductivity, microscopic pairing mechanisms, and superconducting heterostructures.

Quantum magnetism and spintronics

We are interested in spin transport, magnon generation, superconducting spintronics, and the coupling between magnetic and superconducting systems.

Topological quantum matter

Our work also includes topological phases of matter, higher-order topological insulators and superconductors, and the relation between topology and experimentally observable boundary phenomena.

Disordered and amorphous systems

A further direction concerns electronic structure, multifractality, and interaction effects in disordered and amorphous quantum materials.
