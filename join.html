Postdoctoral positions

Postdoctoral positions are currently available through the KFUPM Quantum Center. The positions offer substantial scientific independence together with close collaboration across the Center. Relevant directions include quantum condensed matter theory, topology, magnetism and spintronics, quantum many-body physics, and related areas.

Appointments are initially for 24 months, with the possibility of extension, and the start date is flexible.

Learn more and apply here!

Graduate and PhD student positions

Graduate and PhD student positions are available at KFUPM for distinguished applicants. If you are interested in joining the group as a PhD student, write me an e-mail to learn more. Please attach your CV and a few sentences about your research interests.

Undergraduate and graduate research

Graduate and undergraduate students who are interested in research projects are also very welcome to get in touch. Send me an e-mail, or simply come by my office (6-142) to talk.
