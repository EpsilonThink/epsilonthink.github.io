Even Thingstad

Assistant Professor of Physics

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist working on superconductivity, quantum magnetism, topological phases, and engineered quantum materials.

I am currently based at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM).

Research interests

Superconductivity

Interfacial and unconventional superconductivity, pairing mechanisms, and engineered superconducting systems.

Quantum magnetism

Spin transport, magnonics, superconducting spintronics, and magnetic quantum systems.

Topological matter

Topological phases, higher-order topology, and related phenomena in quantum materials.
